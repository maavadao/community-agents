# Agents

One folder per agent. Start from [`_template`](_template).

The founding five. Three cover the full content lifecycle and work as a chain; two support schools and organisations.

| Agent | What it does | Maintainer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Content Research Agent](content-research-agent) | Content lifecycle 1/3: sources, angles and an outline with citations | maavaDao team |
| [Content Writer Agent](content-writer-agent) | Content lifecycle 2/3: an outline becomes a first draft | maavaDao team |
| [Content Review Agent](content-review-agent) | Content lifecycle 3/3: fact, tone, accessibility and copyright checks before publishing | maavaDao team |
| [Compliance Agent](compliance-agent) | Checks policies and forms against safeguarding and data-protection checklists | maavaDao team |
| [Law Agent](law-agent) | Explains laws and rights in plain language, with sources | maavaDao team |
